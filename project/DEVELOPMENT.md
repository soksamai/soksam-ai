# Development workflow

SokSam AI is built with a hybrid engineering workflow that combines conventional software development with AI-assisted coding.

## Human + AI collaboration

The current public description of the workflow is approximately:

- **~50% direct human work by Viktor** — architecture, product decisions, implementation, debugging, integration, testing and final review;
- **~50% AI-assisted work** — code generation, refactoring, implementation suggestions, research support, repetitive work and acceleration of complex tasks.

The ratio is approximate and can change from feature to feature. Some parts are mostly written manually, while other parts use substantially more AI assistance.

## What “vibe coding” means here

AI is used as a development tool, not as an autonomous owner of the project. Complex scripts, backend integrations, authentication flows, model-routing logic and production changes are still reviewed, integrated and tested by the developer before they become part of SokSam AI.

The project therefore combines rapid AI-assisted iteration with direct engineering responsibility.

## Current team model

SokSam AI currently has one primary developer, **Viktor**. The project may expand its contributor or team structure later as the product grows.
